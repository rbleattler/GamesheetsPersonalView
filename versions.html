Version history

Every major iteration remains available so you can compare the direction of the project — or simply use the version you prefer.

V3.6
First-run onboarding, Help, and feedbackNew visitors now choose their league up front. Added a persistent Help button with FAQ access plus a friendly in-app feedback builder that opens a pre-filled GitHub issue.
Open V3.6
V3.5
Searchable league and season discoveryAdded a local public-season catalog with type-ahead search, strong name/token matching, cautious acronym inference, current-season ranking, sport filtering, and a direct URL/ID fallback.
Open V3.5
V3.4.2
Clearer Add Players selectionReplaced the ambiguous star-only picker with explicit multi-select checkboxes, selection counts, Select all/Clear controls, and an Add Players confirmation action.
Open V3.4.2
V3.4.1
Natural division orderingMinor UX refinement: division pickers now sort by age, then competitive tier, then conference so team selection reads naturally on mobile.
Open V3.4.1
V3.4
Multiple teams and smarter schedule cleanupMy Teams now supports multiple kids/teams with division-first selection and per-team rosters. Mobile box scores fit without unnecessary scrolling, and stale Live games older than 24 hours are hidden by default with an opt-in Settings toggle.
Open V3.4
V3.3
Compact scoreboard and timeline play-by-playMoved SOG/PIM into the main score card, redesigned Summary as a period-grouped event timeline with expandable goal/penalty details, and added a dedicated box-score tab.
Open V3.3
V3.2.3
Penalty totals and chronological game eventsCorrected scoring/penalty ordering to period + countdown time, restored team PIM totals in game summaries, and improved penalty-side detection for more accurate team totals.
Open V3.2.3
V3.2.2
Player event details, appearance settings, and polishExpandable goal/assist events in player game history, GameSheet links across game views, light/dark/device appearance, clear-all-data controls, and mobile alignment fixes.
Open V3.2.2
V3.2.1
Roster accuracy and clearer game actionsMy Team roster membership moved to actual game lineups instead of assuming missing team IDs. Scheduled venue games gained an internal Details view instead of unexpectedly opening GameSheet.
Open V3.2.1
V3.2
Player profiles and connected statsBrowse the My Team roster, open player detail cards from names throughout the app, drill into player season stats, make Recent Results interactive, and identify teams in the scoring summary.
Open V3.2
V3.1
MyHockeyHub identity and major reliability fixesRebranded away from GameSheets+, moved league selection into Settings, tightened schedule filtering, improved Add Players, added internal game details, and added the independent-project disclaimer.
Open V3.1
V3
Personalization becomes the product modelIntroduced the mobile-first My Team, Venues, Players, and Schedule destinations. Saved preferences became useful views rather than just highlights.
Open V3
V2
Visual redesign and richer game statsIntroduced the HockeyHub-style card design, team logos with fallbacks, stronger live-score presentation, and an in-app Game Stats drawer.
Open V2
V1
The original public schedule viewerThe proof of concept: load the public league schedule, search and filter it, favorite rinks, choose My Team, inspect rink-day schedules, and make the huge GameSheet season feed practical on a phone.
